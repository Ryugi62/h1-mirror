H1 Mirror loading the Python engine…

India's chemists already log every Schedule H1 antibiotic sale. H1 Mirror reads those lines and sends each registered prescriber a private monthly mirror: their Access / Watch / Reserve mix against same-specialty peers. This page runs the same Python engine as the repository (engine/domain + engine/application) inside your browser via Pyodide. Nothing you type leaves this page.

1 · Classify lines as written on an Indian bill or H1 register

One line per row: brand or generic name, strength and form. Brand → molecule → WHO AWaRe 2023 group, plus WHO's list of not-recommended fixed-dose combinations. A fixed rule table decides; no AI in this step.

Unknown brand → “Unclassified” until the chemist maps it once.

2 · One month of lines → private letters + district view

Load the synthetic sample month (12 pharmacies: 10 export complete bills, 2 only photograph their handwritten H1 register; 30 registered prescribers; unregistered providers; a few misread registration numbers) or your own CSV with columns date, pharmacy, prescriber_reg, specialty, drug_line, qty[, source]. What the number means: Watch + Reserve share = Watch + Reserve lines ÷ all WHO-classified antibiotic lines of one prescriber, counted only from complete bill exports (the H1 register alone holds mostly Watch drugs, so photo rows feed the district aggregate only). A letter needs ≥20 such lines and a registration number found on the council list; peers = same specialty. The district sees aggregates only when ≥10 pharmacies contribute.

download the sample CSV

3 · The chemist's side: a printable register, not extra work

Each chemist sees only their own month: every antibiotic line from bills and confirmed register rows, with its WHO group, ready to print for the drug inspector. Patient details stay on the chemist's own copy. No other pharmacy's data, and the district never gets a per-pharmacy report.
